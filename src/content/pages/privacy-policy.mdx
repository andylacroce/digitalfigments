title: "Privacy Policy"
---
Digital Figments is a personal photo/video/music blog run by Andy Lacroce. It doesn't have user accounts, comments, or a contact form, and it doesn't run any analytics or advertising scripts.

## Hosting

This site is hosted on Vercel. Like any web host, Vercel's infrastructure logs standard request data (e.g. IP address, browser type, page requested) for operational and security purposes. This site itself doesn't access or store that data.

## Local storage

Your light/dark theme choice is saved in your browser's local storage so it's remembered on your next visit. This stays on your device — it's never sent to this site or anyone else.

## Fonts

Page text is displayed using Google Fonts, loaded directly from Google's servers. Loading a font this way sends a request to Google that may include your IP address, per [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Embedded content

Some pages (the covers/demos pages) embed video from other sites, such as YouTube. Embedded content behaves in the exact same way as if you'd visited that site directly — the site hosting it may collect data about you, use cookies, and track your interaction with the embedded content according to its own privacy policy.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time as the site changes. Check back here for the current version.
