January 31, 2001
           Sorry this page is up and down so much but the university keeps cappin' my bandwidth and I keep getting shut down. Anyhow, school has consumed my life but I'm still rockin. I was sitting bored in chemistry recitation one day so I started to write lyrics for some random riffs when I started to think about certain aspects of home and my life that will never be the same (Time is indeed like a river). I then began to write this song tenatively titled "Emerald in the Sky" and realized that a pretty riff I figured out awhile ago would go perfectly with a good piano part. I'm taking a piano class now at school so I should be able to come up with something good by the end of the semester. The song will probably be pretty long (5-9 min.) because I have this image of it being like a "November Rain" (don't laugh, it doesnt sound like it at all... I mean length and meaning wise). It is about a friend we lost and will never forget because in losing her I think we all pulled a bit closer together. Even if we all go our separate ways (we've already become a bit estranged, I'll be the first to admit that) we'll never forget our class and the wonderful times we had together. The song is a story of those times, good and bad, and an analysis of how we all were changed by various things. Don't laugh, but I've always seen myself performing a song like this on the piano on stage (like Axl :) ). You all laugh at my obsession with Guns N' Roses, but I'm going to explain now why I love them. They rocked out old school metal songs but weren't afraid to write songs and ballads that were true to life and all the changes they endured. When you listen to Axl, you can tell that he cares about the music and nothing else. Slash's solos let you feel the emotion of the songs without words, something very few guitarists have done throughout history. They had songs about the drugs, alcohol, and the women of a stereotypical rock band, but also wrote about love, loss, happiness, and also sorrow. Even the dichotomy of a name like Guns N' Roses says alot about what they were out to accomplish. Like them or hate them, they're not just some 80's hair band that time will forget... to me the are legends. Viva la GNR.
