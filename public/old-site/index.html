Bad Equipment was a three piece band from Collingswood, NJ. The music could only be described as entertaining. We were probably the worst band ever but when we went up on stage at Drewstock we put on quite a display. The band was influenced by Faith No More, Mr. Bungle, Guns n' Roses, Weezer, Foo Fighters, the Deftones, Reverie, and Again Forgotten. Yet, we didn't sound the least bit like any of these bands (even though we did cover/butcher some of their stuff). When the band split, Adams and Boyle formed their own punk band called Clark W, and I was lucky enough to be taken in by some friends in Reverie (the greatest rock band of all time). I am now attending an out-of-state college and estranged from the artists formerly known as Reverie.

Update: Now out of college and working for the man... and the man don't like rock n' roll.
